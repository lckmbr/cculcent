01 Who We Are
Who We Are

For decades, the city and territory of Beni in eastern Democratic Republic Of Congo have endured armed conflict, displacement, and violence that have deprived thousands of young people of the opportunities they deserve. But Beni is more than conflict. Beyond the hardships lies a generation of talented, creative, resilient, and ambitious young people eager to learn, create, innovate, serve their communities, and contribute to the future of their country.

Our answer is culture.

UJAMAA was founded by Rogatien Milord, a Congolese artist, cultural entrepreneur, and philanthropist from Beni.

05 Region
Where We Work

Beni, North Kivu

Beni lies in North Kivu, in eastern Democratic Republic Of Congo, immediately west of Virunga National Park and the Rwenzori Mountains, on the edge of the Ituri Forest. It is a region of striking natural beauty — and one that has carried the weight of conflict for decades. UJAMAA works from the heart of this territory.

Together, we create the space for young people in Beni to choose culture, opportunity and peace.
